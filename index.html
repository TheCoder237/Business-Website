COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the size and functionality of your project. After discussing your needs, I'll provide a proposal with a clear price.

How long does development take?

Timelines depend on the number of pages, requested features, and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Responsive design is part of my website development approach, so the layout is designed to work across phones, tablets, and computers.

Can you update my website after launch?

Yes. Maintenance and additional improvements can be arranged separately.

Do I need to purchase a domain?

A custom domain is recommended for a professional business website. I can help with the setup. Domain and hosting costs will be explained before the project begins.
